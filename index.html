About me

From a small town, building for the web.

I grew up in Tawid, a quiet barangay in Candijay, Bohol, surrounded by mangroves, rice fields and the sea. I started out redesigning posters for school events, then got curious about how websites were made — and never stopped.

Today I run Olalo Studio, a one-person design and development service. I help sari-sari stores, cafés, resorts and student orgs look good online without paying big-city prices.
